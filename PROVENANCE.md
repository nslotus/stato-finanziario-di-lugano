# Provenance

Where the code, the data and the page in this repository come from.

## Origin

| Part | Origin | State taken |
|---|---|---|
| The source readers, sector modules and catalogue (`src/`, `catalog/`) | A private working repository, `lugano-cosmos`, where the sources were located and read sector by sector (October 2026) | Its state of 21 October 2026 (last commit `2a434c2` of 19 October, plus the work of 19–21 October on the department view, the 2025 budget and actual by function and the staff tables) |
| The archived documents and manifest (`sources/`) | The same repository's archive, unchanged: every file still matches its recorded SHA-256 | 74 files, retrieved 14–19 October 2026 |
| Seven upstream data files (`sources/dati-di-lugano@*`) | The public repository [`nslotus/dati-di-lugano`](https://github.com/nslotus/dati-di-lugano), pinned to commits `8c1fd28`, `a90ca4a`, `61e3dbe`, `6627e5f`, `74c57b7` and `969fe8d` | Each file is named after its commit; all six commits are on that repository's `main` branch. On 21 October 2026 each of the seven files was downloaded again from GitHub at its commit and matched its archived SHA-256 |
| The page (`docs/`) | A single-file version shared privately for review, 20–21 October 2026 | Split here into `index.html`, `assets/style.css`, `assets/app.js` and the generated `data/data.js` |
| The source maps (`method/`) | The notes written while each sector's sources were mapped, from the same working repository, including the sections of its README on health, taxes, bond interest and per-taxpayer figures (now `HEALTH-SOURCES.md` and `TAXES-SOURCES.md`) | Design notes removed; see below |

The working repository is private and may be deleted. Everything needed to rebuild and check the figures was brought
over: this repository depends on it for nothing. Not carried over: its other experimental views, its design notes, and
17 documents downloaded while researching that no figure cites (they remain available at their publishers).

## What changed on the way here

- **One pipeline, one page.** The other experimental views of the working repository and their page builders were not
  carried over. `src/site.ts` keeps the data construction and checks of the old page builder and adds what the reviewed
  page showed on top of the sky: each figure's full record, the department staff figures, the overview chapters and the
  yearly totals.
- **No number typed by hand.** In the reviewed page, four notes on the debt carried numbers typed into the page. They are
  now generated by `src/site.ts` from `dati-di-lugano@8c1fd28-debt-profile.json` and the archived 2026 budget release
  (p. 2, the tax multipliers), and each note cites its source. One number of the typed version, a Moody's ratio quoted by
  a newspaper, is not in any archived document and was dropped.
- **Equivalence with the reviewed page.** Before publishing, the data generated here was compared field by field with
  the data embedded in the reviewed page. Every figure, line, department, chapter and English name is identical; 38
  fields differ, all on purpose: the 20 department staff records now carry their number of decimals; the method of the
  municipal-waste figure (Italian and English) no longer names the old project; and the four debt notes (16 fields) are
  now generated with their sources. Their numbers are unchanged, except that the 2026 budget note now also gives the
  overall result and net investment it is computed from, and the Moody's ratio was dropped (see above).
- **Reproducible.** The catalogue was dated by the time of the build; it is now dated by its newest source
  (`sourcesAsOf`), so the same sources always give byte-identical files (check 11 of `npm run verify`).
- **Wording.** References to the working repository's other views ("cosmos", "galaxy") were removed from code comments, one
  method text and the source maps; two notes that recorded a choice as a "user decision" now say it was a choice made
  when the source was mapped.
- **Fonts** are served from this repository instead of Google Fonts, so viewing the page sends no request to a third
  party. The page sets a Content Security Policy that allows only its own files.
