# Economic initiatives: source map

Mapped 19 October 2026 with Exa search, then checked by downloading each file and reading the figures locally (phrases located page by page; one figure read from the page image, noted below). Archived so far in `data/raw`; the build will copy the ones used into `sources/`.

The question residents and businesses ask: what does the City spend to grow the economy and help people into work (economic development, digital innovation, Plan ₿, adult training and back-to-work), and what comes out of it. Function 8 "Economia pubblica" in the 2024 accounts is only CHF 4.55M and mixes agriculture, tourism and energy; the initiatives sit in several divisions, so they are mapped one by one.

## Notes on the sources

- **Plan ₿ has no line of its own in the City's accounts or contracts.** In 2023 a council amendment tried to cut CHF 100'000 from the economic office's "special projects and new initiatives" as the estimated Plan ₿ cost (laRegione, 15 Dec 2023); that line was CHF 80'250 in 2025 and is budgeted at 257'200 for 2027, without a breakdown. Proposal: show what is documented (the City's own spending on the economic office and Living Lab, Tether's in-kind commitment and the MoU's no-obligation clause) and state that a Plan ₿ cost is not published.
- **Plan ₿ results.** The Municipio answered a council question in January 2026 (about a hundred blockchain companies registered since 2022, about CHF 7M invested by funds in Swiss start-ups including two local ones, some thirty companies in the private hub, tax revenue "limited" and not yet quantifiable). Only press reports were found (laRegione, 30 Jan 2026); the official answer is not yet archived, so these figures are not used.
- **The work sector shares a cost centre with youth outreach** (5121); the accounts do not split them, and neither do the LuganoNetWork reports give a cost. Proposal: show outcomes (people placed, courses, participants) with the cost of the whole centre and say that it includes youth outreach.
- **The placement series changes its definitions**: "collocamenti" (2022–2023, jobs plus internships plus apprenticeships), "assunzioni" (2024–2025). Shown as published, with the breakdown, not as one trend line.
- **The contracts list covers direct and invited awards only**, above CHF 5'000; open tenders (on simap.ch) and payments to foundations or other public bodies are not in it. A 69xx postcode means the supplier's address is in the Lugano area, not necessarily in the City. This answers part of data-wanted issue #6.
- **MyLugano app users** (13'000 active wallets at end 2023) appear only in the press; not used.
- **Not ranked against other municipalities**: no comparable figures exist for the other 105.
