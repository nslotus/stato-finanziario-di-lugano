# Culture and sport: source map

Mapped 18 October 2026, with Exa search, then checked by downloading each file and reading the figures locally (phrases located by page).

Culture, sport, leisure and church is function 3 of the municipal accounts. In 2024 the City spent **CHF 53.6M** on it (Ledger spending chapter, `3 Cultura, sport, tempo libero, chiesa`), about **CHF 843 per resident, 3rd in Ticino** (median about 157; Chiasso and Ascona higher). On museums, concerts and theatres and other culture alone (311 + 322 + 329) Lugano is **1st in Ticino** (CHF 479 per resident, median 22): the City carries institutions that serve the whole region (LAC, MASI).

## Notes on the sources

- **Who runs what.** LAC is an autonomous body of the City (like LIS for social services). MASI is a foundation of Canton and City, funded by both (City 3.3M, Canton 3.4M in 2025) and by the Confederation; it is marked as not run by the City alone. Teatro Foce is the City's.
- **Visitors fell in 2025** at the MASI (LAC −22.7%, Palazzo Reali −36.9%) after the 2024 Calder exhibition; the yearbook gives the figures without causes, and the star will not add one.
- **LongLake figures are estimates** (events × venue capacity), as the yearbook says; shown with that label, not added to ticketed attendance.
- **The stadium bond.** The City chose to finance the stadium purchase with a bond (source 6). Our bond list has a CHF 100M bond issued in August 2025, but no source found ties a specific bond to the stadium, so they are not linked.
- **PSE credits still to source as files.** The indoor arena (PalaRaiffeisen) purchase credit of CHF 80.285M (MM 12552, 13 November 2025) and the Centro sportivo al Maglio credits (37.21M, MM 10775; supplementary 7.415M, MM 12253) are listed on the City's PSE page (HTML, not archived by rule); their press releases or messages are needed as PDFs before they become figures.
- **Sport users fell 7.9% in 2025** mainly because the Carona pool closed (yearbook).
- **Comparisons.** Function 3 and its lines rank across the 106 municipalities (source 1). Attendance and visitor figures are Lugano-only.
