# Missing datasets: what residents and businesses cannot yet check

Draft, 19 October 2026; updated 10 November 2026 with the searches of 1–2 and 10 November, each also posted on its GitHub issue. Prompted by the debate in Ticino's business community ahead of the April elections: which public tasks are still needed, which obligations can go, which functions can merge, whether subsidies and concessions to companies pay off, and what will change, by when, with what results. Each item below starts from one of those questions, says what the published data can already show from published sources, and names the dataset that would answer it. "Not found" means not found in the documents archived so far, not that it does not exist.

## 1. Which tasks are still needed: staff against workload

- **Already shown:** staff by department and cost centre, 2025 actual and 2026–2027 budgets (2027 budget message, tables 1 and 1a): 1'301 permanent full-time units, CHF 114.4M gross salaries; e.g. building permits (Edilizia privata) 22.7 units, roads 26.1, population registry 15.5.
- **Found on 10 November 2026:**
  - *Staff, four years.* The 2025 accounts (pp. 76–77, table 1) give each centre's permanent full-time units for the 2025 actual, the 2025 budget and the 2024 actual (e.g. Edilizia privata 22.73, 25.00, 23.72), and table 5 (pp. 85–86) the reason for each gap from the budget, mostly delayed or forgone replacements. With the 2027 budget message, staff is published for 2024–2027.
  - *Counts of work, for some services.* The cost-centre pages of the 2025 accounts print activity counts, usually 2025 with 2024 beside it: building permits issued (1'321 in 2025, 1'386 in 2024, p. 226) and permits to start works, habitability; police interventions (8'037) and people dealt with (16'558, p. 174), traffic, speed and alcohol controls (pp. 175–176, with series from 2021 in the police report 2025, pp. 40–42); the operations room's events (17'498) and calls (172'483, p. 177); fire-brigade rescues (1'599) and services to the population (2'200, p. 178; a chart from 2012 on p. 181, printed as an image); the social office's files and AVS procedures (pp. 197–198); waste by type, 2020–2025 (p. 167); events at the Palazzo dei Congressi, 2023–2025 (p. 217). Pupils and sections come from the school-year releases and *Lugano in cifre 2026*; assistance cases since 2014, swimming-pool and theatre visitors from *Lugano in cifre 2026*.
  - *Where the City ties staff to work itself.* The police operations room was set at 20 units in 2019 and "ridotto stabilmente a 19 dal 2025 per ragioni di risparmio", which "ha ridotto il margine necessario a garantire un'adeguata continuità operativa 24 ore su 24" (2027 budget message, p. 126). Building permits keep "15 architetti e ingegneri" for "circa 1'300 domande di costruzione all'anno" (p. 180). The social office reports "crescente pressione operativa" at the counters (2025 accounts, p. 197).
  - *Services with no count at all,* although large: cleaning (50.00 units), roads and hill zones (26.10 + 43.74), building maintenance and management (21.30 + 14.59), finance and tax offices (17.27 + 12.23), legal affairs, planning, the regional protection authority, the police administrative section.
  - *Figures that disagree between City documents.* Road accidents in 2025: 641 in the 2025 accounts (p. 175) and the police report, 675 in *Lugano in cifre 2026* (p. 65); both give 729 for 2024. Applications under the social-benefits regulation: 433 → 312 (2025 accounts, p. 197), 427 → 287 (*Lugano in cifre 2026*, p. 55).
  - *The review of tasks.* The 2025 budget message (p. 19) announced a working group on "la revisione ... del perimetro di attività dell'Amministrazione"; the 2027 budget slides (p. 18) and release (p. 2) place the first efficiency measures in December 2026 and the review of the City's fields of action in 2027. Nothing is published yet.
- **Missing:** a count of the work for the large services that print none; for the others, the same count every year over several years, tied to the cost centre (most counts cover only 2024 and 2025, and some are given only as a percentage change); the review of the "perimetro di attività", with the list of tasks it keeps, changes or drops.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Divisions; Human resources; Comitato di risanamento finanziario). Issue [#1](https://github.com/nslotus/stato-finanziario-di-lugano/issues/1).

## 2. Which obligations can go: mandated vs discretionary spending

- **Already shown:** some spending is fixed by cantonal law, e.g. the contribution to cantonal social costs (CHF 36.0M, LaLPC art. 33) and intercommunal equalisation (CHF 31.0M, function 930).
- **Found on 2 November 2026:** the City states a share but publishes no breakdown. On 25 November 2024 the Municipio told the City Council that 60% of spending is "indotte da basi legali superiori" (official minutes, p. 2), and announced a mapping of every service against a matrix it had approved; the press release of 6 February 2026 announced a systematic review of the "perimetro delle attività comunali". The 2025 accounts, the 2027 budget message and the financial plan classify spending by function and by type, never by legal basis. The cantonal *Ticino 2020* reform inventoried about seventy Canton–municipality flows with their legal basis; that is not the City's spending.
- **Missing:** the City's spending (and revenue) by account or cost centre, tagged federal law, cantonal law, City regulation or decision of the Municipio, with the norm; the 2024 mapping of services, or the figures behind the 60%.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Finance), Canton (Sezione degli enti locali). Issue [#2](https://github.com/nslotus/stato-finanziario-di-lugano/issues/2).

## 3. Which functions can merge: intercommunal agreements

- **Already shown:** police region III (44 municipalities, about 157'000 residents), the regional protection authority.
- **Found on 2 November 2026:** the 2025 accounts (p. 33, "Consorzi") list 11 consortia with their legal basis, purpose, members, the City's voting rights and its key for current costs, from 2.00% (Basso Ceresio river banks) to 65.638% (civil protection, Regione di Lugano Città). The table gives shares, not francs; several amounts are on the transfer lines (pp. 47–48), e.g. civil protection CHF 1'962'062.43. The page had called that line a fire-brigade consortium: it is civil protection, and is corrected. The civil-protection row uses a population of 63'495 at 31.12.2024; the cantonal statistics give 63'629 (asked on the issue).
- **Missing:** the intermunicipal agreements (*convenzioni*): services the City provides to other municipalities or receives from them, with partners, costs and revenue; for each consortium, the franc amount next to its share.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano; Canton (register of consortia). Issue [#3](https://github.com/nslotus/stato-finanziario-di-lugano/issues/3).

## 4. Do subsidies and concessions to companies pay off

- **Already shown:** transfers (account 36) are the City's largest type of spending, **CHF 200.9M in 2024, 41% of the total** (canton annex, tab9a); contributions to the main cultural institutions are listed by name in the 2027 budget message; AIL dividends 11–12M a year.
- **Found on 1–2 November 2026:** groups 361 and 363 are itemised by account in the 2025 accounts (pp. 47–48) and the 2027 budget message (pp. 33–34), and many lines name the recipient: the Canton (AVS/AI/PC 39.0M, assistance 5.5M, financial recovery 5.6M), Comunità tariffale 6.2M, the consortia, Croce Verde 1.2M, TPL, the USI faculties 0.63M, the cultural institutions. The largest line with no recipient is care-home financing (account 36321000): CHF 39.2M in 2025, 43.7M in the 2027 budget; the 2027 message (p. 35) explains it by days of residence and cost per day, so it is paid per home, but no home is named. The next is home care (SPITEX), CHF 12.5M in 2025. Concessions (group 41) were CHF 6.83M in 2025, in six fee types; only SGA is named. On p. 51 of the 2025 accounts four concession accounts carry tax labels (asked on the issue). Municipal message 11719 sets CHF 343'000 a year for three filarmoniche, while account 36363006 prints 305'500 (asked on the issue).
- **Missing:**
  - care-home and SPITEX financing by institution;
  - every other contribution by recipient, amount, purpose and duration;
  - the economic-promotion measures (LuganoNetwork, Living Lab, Plan ₿, start-up support) with budget, beneficiaries, stated targets and results;
  - the concessions the City grants (public ground, energy, harbours), by holder and fee.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Sviluppo economico, Finance); Canton for tax relief to companies and cantonal economic promotion, which is not published by company or municipality. Issue [#4](https://github.com/nslotus/stato-finanziario-di-lugano/issues/4).

## 5. What will change, by when: the recovery plan and the investment plan

- **Already shown:** results and debt over time; net investment 2024 CHF 75.5M, 2025 74.1M, **2026 budget 262.4M** (as restated in the 2027 budget message; the 2026 budget release gave 263.5M), 2027 budget 97.0M; the 22 assets to sell, named in the financial plan 2026–2033 (pp. 5–8, total proceeds 306'073'625).
- **Found on 1 November 2026:** the work-by-work register is published, in two documents. The 2027 budget message (annex "Conti investimenti", pp. 313–337) lists 126 works voted by the Council, 11 within the Municipio's authority, 7 licensed messages and 122 in planning, with credit, probable spending to 31.12.2026, budget 2027, plan 2028–2030 and a 2031–2034 remainder. The 2025 accounts (pp. 385–415) add each work's progress (%) and spending at 31.12.2025. Both are now on the page (the star "Credito d'investimento non ancora speso"): at 31.12.2025, 244 open works with CHF 540.9M of credit not yet spent. Two questions on the register as printed are on the issue (income subtotals on pp. 407–411; progress figures that do not match spending).
- **Missing:**
  - the CHF 22.9M of recovery measures, with target, deadline and progress each year: the plan prints "manovra correttiva CHF 22.9 mio non dettagliata" (p. 11), and the City expects a first list in December 2026;
  - one register joining credit, spending, progress and schedule for the same works, updated after the 2025 accounts.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Municipio, Finance, Immobili). Issue [#5](https://github.com/nslotus/stato-finanziario-di-lugano/issues/5).

## 6. Conditions for business: permits, procurement, company base

- **Already shown:** companies and legal forms in Lugano (Zefix), company tax (CHF 75.6M in 2024) and profit per legal entity (ESTV 2022), jobs per resident, vacancy rate.
- **Found on 2 November 2026:** building permits issued, 1'321 in 2025 and 1'386 in 2024 (2025 accounts, p. 226), by procedure; the four parts add up to 1'331, and "11 preliminary (+54.5%)" cannot follow from a whole number in 2024 (both asked on the issue). The City's list of 2025 contracts of CHF 5'000 and more (2'041 rows, with date, procedure, subject, supplier and amount) is published and archived.
- **Missing:**
  - processing times (days from application to decision) for permits and licences;
  - contracts under CHF 5'000, and lists for earlier years in one place;
  - business demography at municipal level: companies founded, moved in, moved out and closed each year.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Edilizia privata, Procurement); BFS/USTAT for business demography. Issue [#6](https://github.com/nslotus/stato-finanziario-di-lugano/issues/6).

## 7. Training and support for people

- **Already shown:** schools and their pupils; social assistance and supplementary benefits; the LuganoNetWork activity reports 2022–2025 (2025: 50 hires, 587 one-to-one sessions, 34 free adult courses with 286 participants). The reports say *collocamenti* until 2023 and *assunzioni* from 2024, so the series may not count the same thing every year.
- **Found on 2 November 2026:** the services (LuganoNetWork, Spazio lavoro e formazione, Punto digitale, eQuiD) sit in cost centre 5121 *Prossimità e lavoro*, CHF 1.73M in 2025 (2027 budget message, p. 147), which also pays the youth outreach workers. Employment programmes for people on social assistance: 23.72 units and CHF 101'910.75 in 2025, against 35 units and CHF 126'000 budgeted (2025 accounts p. 81; 2027 budget message p. 63); the 2027 message (p. 60) says they are essentially reimbursed by the Canton.
- **Missing:** the cost of each service on its own; people reached and outcomes after 6 and 12 months (still in work, course completed); how many participants live in Lugano. The cantonal statistics on reintegration by municipality have not been checked yet.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Socialità, Sviluppo economico); Canton (Ufficio del sostegno sociale e dell'inserimento). Issue [#7](https://github.com/nslotus/stato-finanziario-di-lugano/issues/7).
