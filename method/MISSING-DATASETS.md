# Missing datasets: what residents and businesses cannot yet check

Draft, 19 October 2026. Prompted by the debate in Ticino's business community ahead of the April elections: which public tasks are still needed, which obligations can go, which functions can merge, whether subsidies and concessions to companies pay off, and what will change, by when, with what results. Each item below starts from one of those questions, says what the published data can already show from published sources, and names the dataset that would answer it. "Not found" means not found in the documents archived so far, not that it does not exist.

## 1. Which tasks are still needed: staff against workload

- **Already shown:** staff by department and cost centre, 2025 actual and 2026–2027 budgets (2027 budget message, tables 1 and 1a): 1'301 permanent full-time units, CHF 114.4M gross salaries; e.g. building permits (Edilizia privata) 22.7 units, roads 26.1, population registry 15.5.
- **Missing:** the workload of each cost centre over several years (permits handled, km of roads maintained, registry procedures, cases per social worker), so that staff can be read against tasks. Only three years of staff are published, in one budget message.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Divisions; Human resources).

## 2. Which obligations can go: mandated vs discretionary spending

- **Already shown:** some spending is fixed by cantonal law, e.g. the contribution to cantonal social costs (CHF 36.0M, LaLPC art. 33) and intercommunal equalisation (CHF 31.0M, function 930).
- **Missing:** the City's spending split by legal basis: federal or cantonal obligation, City regulation, or free choice of the Municipio. Without it, "eliminate unnecessary obligations" cannot be costed.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Finance), Canton (Sezione degli enti locali).

## 3. Which functions can merge: intercommunal agreements

- **Already shown:** single cases found in the accounts and reports: police region III (44 municipalities, about 157'000 residents), the fire-brigade consortium (CHF 1.96M), the regional protection authority.
- **Missing:** a register of the consortia, conventions and agreements the City belongs to, with its share of costs and revenue, the services covered and the partner municipalities.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano; Canton (register of consortia).

## 4. Do subsidies and concessions to companies pay off

- **Already shown:** transfers (account 36) are the City's largest type of spending, **CHF 200.9M in 2024, 41% of the total** (canton annex, tab9a); contributions to the main cultural institutions are listed by name in the 2027 budget message; concession revenue (account 412) is CHF 6.45M; AIL dividends 11–12M a year.
- **Missing:**
  - a register of all City contributions and subsidies by recipient (companies, foundations, associations, events), amount, purpose and duration;
  - the economic-promotion measures (LuganoNetwork, Living Lab, Plan ₿, start-up support) with budget, beneficiaries and stated targets;
  - any evaluation of their results;
  - the concessions the City grants (public ground, energy, harbours), by holder and fee.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Sviluppo economico, Finance); Canton for tax relief to companies and cantonal economic promotion, which is not published by company or municipality.

## 5. What will change, by when: the recovery plan and the investment plan

- **Already shown:** results and debt over time; net investment 2024 CHF 75.5M, 2025 74.1M, **2026 budget 263.5M**, 2027 budget 97.0M.
- **Missing:**
  - the measures of the financial recovery plan the Municipio approved (cited in the 2027 budget message, p. 57), with savings target, deadline and progress each year;
  - a project-level investment register (credit approved, spent to date, schedule, status), so the gap between budgeted and realised investment can be followed project by project.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Municipio, Finance, Immobili).

## 6. Conditions for business: permits, procurement, company base

- **Already shown:** companies and legal forms in Lugano (Zefix), company tax (CHF 75.6M) and profit per legal entity (ESTV 2022), jobs per resident, vacancy rate.
- **Missing:**
  - building permits and business licences: applications, decisions and processing times per year;
  - public procurement: contracts awarded by the City and its companies, with amount, procedure and the supplier's location (simap.ch carries tenders above the thresholds, not a complete annual list);
  - business demography at municipal level: companies founded, moved in, moved out and closed each year.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Edilizia privata, Procurement); BFS/USTAT for business demography.

## 7. Training and support for people

- **Already shown:** schools and their pupils; social assistance and supplementary benefits.
- **Missing:** what the City spends on adult training, upskilling and back-to-work programmes, and how many people they reach; the 2027 budget message lists employment programmes (35 units, CHF 126'000 in the 2027 budget) but no multi-year series or outcomes.
- **Holder:** City of Lugano (Socialità, Sviluppo economico); Canton (Ufficio del sostegno sociale e dell'inserimento).
