# Debt and investment outlook: source map

Mapped 19 October 2026 with Exa search, then checked by downloading the file and reading the figures locally. Archived so far in `data/raw`.

The question: where is the City's debt heading, what will it invest, and what does the plan count on to bring the debt down? The page already shows the debt today (1'049.9M at end 2025, 1'123.4M at Q1 2026), three plan points from the City's press release, and net investment budgeted for 2026 and 2027. What was missing is the plan itself, year by year, and what it depends on.

## Notes on the sources

- **The plan is not approved by the Council.** Under LOC art. 152 it is presented "for information and discussion" (p. 16); it is the Municipio's intention, revised every year with the budget.
- **The debt reduction rests on two things not yet done**: selling 22 assets for 306.1M, most of it in 2031–2033 (the Alpiq stake alone is 104.4M in 2033), and recovery measures of 22.9M a year that the plan does not detail ("manovra correttiva non dettagliata", p. 11). Without them the debt would not fall after 2030. The cards say so plainly.
- **Two recaps**: without and with asset sales (p. 3 and p. 9). The global result with sales turns positive in 2029 (+110.7M in the press release) because of accounting revaluations; the debt table uses the cash proceeds. The page uses the recap without sales for results and the debt table for the debt, as the plan itself does.
- **The plan is already a year old**: the 2027 budget moved the Palazzetto payment and postponed works, so 2027–2030 investment is 51.4M higher than planned. Shown next to the plan, with the City's explanation.
- **Rounding between City documents**: the 2027 budget message gives the plan's 2027 result as −19.4M and the budget's global result as −80.0M (p. 20); the press release and slides give −19.5M and −80.4M. The plan itself prints −19'446'211 (p. 3); the budget's ricapitolazione gives the global result. The build reads both from their tables, not from the text.
- **Not ranked against other municipalities**: financial plans are not collected by the canton.
