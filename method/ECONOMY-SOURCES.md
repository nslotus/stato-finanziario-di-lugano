## Notes on the sources

- **Jobs are counted where they are**, not where workers live: 58'680 jobs for 63'629 residents (about 92 per 100 residents) shows Lugano as a job centre for the region. Cross-border workers (source 2) and STATENT employees (source 1) are different statistics and are not combined into a share.
- **Unemployment per municipality**: SECO's data go down to the municipality, but only inside the interactive AMSTAT application (amstat.ch); no downloadable table per Ticino municipality was found (USTAT's labour-market panorama stops at the canton). The star stays Lugano-only, with Ticino and Switzerland as context.
- **Public companies (446)**: the annex does not name companies. AIL's report (source 8) gives its dividends to the City. The dividend on a year's profit is decided the following June, and neither source says in which year of the City's accounts it is booked. So the dividend is shown next to account 446, not subtracted from it.
- **Boundaries**: the newest USTAT sheets use the 2025 municipalities (100); our register is 2024 (106). Lugano's boundaries did not change; Ticino comparisons use each table's own set of municipalities and say so.

Notes:
- **Sole proprietorships** must register only from CHF 100'000 in turnover (art. 931 CO), so the register undercounts them; they are taxed as individuals, inside the personal-tax star (400). SA and Sagl pay company tax, already shown at three levels (401, IC·PG, IFD·Q).
- **Boundaries.** The register uses the 2025 municipalities, matched exactly to the 2025 population. The ESTV file uses the 2022 tax units (110, some since merged); its two measures are per legal person, so no population is needed, and 105 units have complete figures.
- **Tax secrecy.** ESTV withholds figures that would reveal a taxpayer; Ticino's units add up to 30'975 of the canton's 30'982 legal persons. The check allows the units to fall short by at most 0.1%, never to exceed the total.
- **Average profit** is a mean: a few large companies weigh heavily. The star says so; no median per company is published.
